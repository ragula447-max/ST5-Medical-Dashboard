Paracetamol

Analgesic / Antipyretic
Primary Purpose Relief of mild to moderate pain and reduction of fever.
Conditions Headache, fever, muscle pain and common cold symptoms.
Dosage Follow the dosage recommended by a doctor or the product label.
⚠️ Do not exceed the recommended dose. Excessive use can damage the liver.

Ibuprofen

NSAID
Primary Purpose Helps reduce pain, inflammation and fever.
Conditions Muscle pain, dental pain, menstrual pain and arthritis.
Dosage Use only according to medical advice or the product label.
⚠️ May cause stomach irritation. Ask a healthcare professional if you have stomach, kidney or heart problems.

Cetirizine

Antihistamine
Primary Purpose Helps relieve allergy symptoms.
Conditions Sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes and hives.
Dosage Follow the dosage recommended by a healthcare professional.
⚠️ May cause drowsiness in some people. Avoid driving if affected.

Omeprazole

Proton Pump Inhibitor
Primary Purpose Reduces the amount of acid produced in the stomach.
Conditions Acid reflux, heartburn and certain stomach ulcers.
Dosage Take according to the prescription or product label.
⚠️ Long-term use should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

Metformin

Antidiabetic Medicine
Primary Purpose Helps control blood glucose levels in type 2 diabetes.
Conditions Mainly used for type 2 diabetes under medical supervision.
Dosage Dosage must be determined by the prescribing doctor.
⚠️ Prescription medicine. Do not start or change the dose without medical advice.

Amoxicillin

Antibiotic
Primary Purpose Treats certain bacterial infections.
Conditions Used for certain respiratory, ear, skin and other bacterial infections.
Dosage Take exactly as prescribed by a healthcare professional.
⚠️ Antibiotics do not treat viral infections. Complete the prescribed course unless your doctor advises otherwise.
