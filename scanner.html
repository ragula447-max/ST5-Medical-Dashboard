📋 How to Scan

  1. Place the medicine on a clear, well-lit surface.
  2. Make sure the tablet or medicine package is clearly visible.
  3. Click Start Camera or upload an image.
  4. Capture or select the image.
  5. Click Identify Medicine.
  6. Always verify the result before taking any medicine.
